Sweet Faces - FAQ and contact

Sweet Faces is a dessert-inspired watch face for round Garmin watches (Connect IQ) with six themes: Red Velvet, Cinnamon Bun, Cupcake, Pistachio Dream, Chocolate Fondant and Lemon Tart. It is bought once in the Garmin Connect IQ Store and every theme and option is included.

Which watches are supported?

Round AMOLED Garmin watches listed on the Connect IQ Store page of the app. The older 64 color MIP watches are not supported because the artwork does not look good on them.

What can I change in the settings?

The theme, the colon after the hour, the weather at the top (off, current temperature, or current temperature with today's low and high), an optional line with the chance of rain under the temperature, the watch battery at the top, and what each of the four pills shows: heart rate, steps, energy (Body Battery), calories, distance, floors climbed, rain chance, temperature, watch battery or stress.

Does it work with the always-on display?

Yes. When the screen dims, the watch face switches to a simple black screen with only the time, the date and the battery level, and moves it slightly every minute to protect the AMOLED screen. Turn always-on on or off in the watch's own display settings.

Refunds and purchase problems

The purchase is made in the Garmin Connect IQ Store, so refunds and purchase questions go through Garmin. For anything about the watch face itself, write to the contact below.

Privacy

See the privacy policy. In short: no health data or location leaves your watch, and the app does not use the internet.
