Sweet Faces - FAQ and contact

Sweet Faces is a dessert-inspired watch face for round Garmin watches (Connect IQ) with seven themes: Lemon Tart, Red Velvet, Cinnamon Kiss, Cupcake, Pistachio Dream, Chocolate Fondant and Macaron Delight. It is bought once in the Garmin Connect IQ Store and every theme and option is included.

Which watches are supported?

Round Garmin watches listed on the Connect IQ Store page of the app: AMOLED watches (Venu, vívoactive, Forerunner, epix, fēnix, MARQ, D2, Descent, Approach, Instinct 3 AMOLED) and the round 64-color watches (fēnix 7/8/9 Solar, Enduro, Forerunner 255/955), where the pictures are adapted to the 64 colors.

What can I change in the settings?

Everything can be set on the watch (Watch Face > Customize) or in Garmin Connect:

  • The theme
  • Colors: the colors of the theme, or Colorful (every value in its own bright color)
  • What each of the four pills shows: heart rate, steps, energy (Body Battery), calories, distance, floors climbed, stress, intensity minutes, respiration, Pulse Ox, notifications, alarms, altitude, rain chance, temperature, watch battery, or None to hide it
  • Two small values under the pills (watch battery and notifications by default), or None
  • The weather at the top (off by default): current temperature, with today's low and high if you like, and the chance of rain
  • The date format (seven formats), 12 or 24 hour time, and the colon after the hour
  • The always-on screen with or without the four values

What happens when I touch and hold a value?

On watches that support it, touching and holding a pill, the weather or a small value opens Garmin's own screen for that value (for example heart rate, steps, Body Battery or weather).

Does it work with the always-on display?

Yes. When the screen dims, the watch face switches to a simple black screen with the time, the date and the battery level (and, if you turn it on in the settings, the four pill values), and moves it slightly every minute to protect the AMOLED screen. Turn always-on on or off in the watch's own display settings.

Refunds and purchase problems

The purchase is made in the Garmin Connect IQ Store, so refunds and purchase questions go through Garmin. For anything about the watch face itself, write to the contact below.
