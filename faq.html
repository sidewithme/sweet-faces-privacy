Sweet Faces - FAQ and contact

Sweet Faces is a dessert-inspired watch face for round Garmin watches (Connect IQ) with six themes: Red Velvet, Cinnamon Bun, Cupcake, Pistachio Dream, Chocolate Fondant and Lemon Tart. It is free to try; one payment through KiezelPay unlocks all themes and options.

How does the trial and the payment work?

Change any setting in Garmin Connect and a free trial starts. When it ends, the watch shows a code and the address kzl.io. Enter the code there to pay once; the watch then unlocks all six themes and every option for good.

I paid but the watch still shows the code

Keep the watch connected to your phone. The watch checks the purchase about every 5 minutes, so it can take a few minutes to unlock. If it still shows the code after 15 minutes, restart the watch face (switch to another one and back) and wait a little longer.

Which watches are supported?

Round AMOLED Garmin watches listed on the Connect IQ Store page of the app. The older 64 color MIP watches are not supported because the artwork does not look good on them.

What can I change in the settings?

The theme, the colon after the hour, the weather at the top (off, current temperature, or current temperature with today's low and high), the watch battery at the top, and what each of the four pills shows: heart rate, steps, energy (Body Battery), calories, distance, floors climbed, rain chance, temperature, watch battery or stress.

Refunds

The payment is handled by KiezelPay (kiezelpay.com), not by Garmin, so Garmin cannot issue refunds for it. Please write to the contact below and include the code shown on your watch.

Privacy

See the privacy policy. In short: no health data or location leaves your watch. After you change a setting, the watch sends KiezelPay its unique device identifier and model number to check the trial and purchase status.
