Sweet Faces - Privacy Policy

Sweet Faces is a watch face for round Garmin watches (Connect IQ).

Sweet Faces does not collect, store or transmit health data, location or personal information. Heart rate, steps, Body Battery, stress, calories, distance, floors, battery and weather are read on the watch from Garmin's own system data and are only used to draw the face. There are no accounts, analytics or advertising.

Sweet Faces is bought once through the Garmin Connect IQ Store; the payment is handled by Garmin and the app does not see it. The app does not use the internet. Settings are stored by Garmin Connect.
